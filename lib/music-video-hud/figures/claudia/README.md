# Claudia: official reference images

**Claudia by anabology** ([claudia.gallery](https://claudia.gallery/)). Claudia is an open character and fan work about Claude: she is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. These three images are hers, not part of this repository's MIT licence; they are here under the
terms of the site, quoted below.

The HUD music video templates (`music-video-hud`, `music-video-hud-elevenlabs`, `music-video-hud-suno`) give them to the image model as
references for the character sheet when the figure is Claudia (WP50, see `lib/music-video-hud/figures.js`). With another figure they are
not used.

## The images

Retrieved on 2026-10-10 from the public API of the site (`https://claudia.gallery/api/images/<id>.json`, the `m` size, 1800 px wide WebP),
scaled down and saved as JPEG (quality 4 of ffmpeg). Nothing else was changed. The images were made with Midjourney v8.2 (AI-generated).

Why these three: they show the constants of the canon the right way round (one clay-orange streak on her right side, the viewer's left; the
star clip on her left side above the ear; the thin headset microphone at her cheek) and an adult face. The two photographs give the
photographic face and skin, the face sheet the head from six angles. Not taken: the canon face `xp-01c-cast.face-gown.4` (the streak and
the clip are mirrored and sunglasses cover the eyes) and `sp-02-open.siren-mcu.2` (dark, wet hair, the streak and the microphone hardly
visible).
