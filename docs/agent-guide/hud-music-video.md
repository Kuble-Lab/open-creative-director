# HUD music videos
Choose a song template, mark lead and backing voices correctly, and review the board before continuing.

1. Call `list_templates`, then `create_from_template` with a matching template ID and the input IDs returned by the tool. The shipped HUD templates expose these node/parameter pairs:

- music-video-hud-elevenlabs: Music video in the HUD style (song by ElevenLabs). Inputs: n25.prompt (Idea for song and video (story, mood, setting)); n28.length (Length of the song (seconds; 60 to 150 fit the default settings)); n26.text (Voice and sound of the song (the default fits Claudia)); n33.influencer (Influencer (no selection: use Figure, default Claudia)); n4.figure (Figure (optional, default: Claudia)); n4.theme (Style); n17.karaoke (Subtitles (karaoke line)); n17.effects (Effects); n4.hud_language (Language of the graphics and the AI label); n31.model (Image model).
- music-video-hud-suno-direct: Music video in the HUD style (song made by Suno). Inputs: n25.prompt (Idea for song and video (story, mood, setting)); n26.text (Singer: voice and persona (default: Claudia)); n1.allow_download_credit (Allow the Suno download credit (needed to fetch the song file)); n33.influencer (Influencer (no selection: use Figure, default Claudia)); n4.figure (Figure (optional, default: Claudia)); n4.theme (Style); n17.karaoke (Subtitles (karaoke line)); n17.effects (Effects); n4.hud_language (Language of the graphics and the AI label); n31.model (Image model).
- music-video-hud-suno: Music video in the HUD style (song by Suno). Inputs: n25.prompt (Idea for song and video (story, mood, setting)); n26.text (Singer: voice and persona (default: Claudia)); n1.asset (Song (after step 1: make it at Suno and upload it here)); n33.influencer (Influencer (no selection: use Figure, default Claudia)); n4.figure (Figure (optional, default: Claudia)); n4.theme (Style); n3.lyrics (Song lyrics (optional, the final text from Suno)); n17.karaoke (Subtitles (karaoke line)); n17.effects (Effects); n4.hud_language (Language of the graphics and the AI label); n31.model (Image model).
- music-video-hud: Music video in the HUD style (your song). Inputs: n1.asset (Song); n4.brief (Idea for the video (a few words are enough; empty: from the song)); n33.influencer (Influencer (no selection: use Figure, default Claudia)); n4.figure (Figure (optional, default: Claudia)); n4.theme (Style); n3.lyrics (Song lyrics (optional; mark a choir with [Choir])); n17.karaoke (Subtitles (karaoke line)); n17.effects (Effects); n4.hud_language (Language of the graphics and the AI label); n31.model (Image model).

music-video-hud-suno prepares a song pack and accepts a separately uploaded song; music-video-hud-suno-direct contains suno.song. The direct-generation template is listed for discovery, not an MCP song-generation route: the MCP Suno tools are read-only. For an existing song, use music-video-hud with `upload_asset` and its asset_id.

2. Supply lyrics as lines with section headers such as [Verse 1] and [Chorus], or a song plan with headers such as [Verse | 20 s]. Square-bracket headers are not sung; plan style lines (+ / -) are not sung. Write every line the lead sings without round parentheses.
3. Choir section names match chor, choir, coros, backing or background vocals (case-insensitive, at a word boundary, also with words after the name): for example [Chor], [Choir], [Coros], [Backing], [Backing Vocals], [Background Vocals]. Line prefixes Chor:, Choir: and Coros: also mark backing voices. [Chorus], [Refrain], [Hook] and singular [Coro] remain lead sections. Choir lines are excluded from lip-sync windows and become story clips.
4. A whole line in round parentheses containing real words normally marks backing vocals; sound-only lines such as (ahhh), and directions such as (x2) or (fade out), are omitted. Exception: if EVERY sung line of a named non-choir section is in parentheses, the parser treats them as lead vocals. Use an explicit choir marker for a guest-only section; parentheses are not a universal no-lip-sync switch.
5. Set theme on music_video.hud_plan: hud (HUD Blue), kuble (Kuble), camcorder (Camcorder) or zine (Zine). music_video.hud_render uses theme: "auto" to follow the plan, or an explicit theme. Review the brief, board, character sheet and scene images through `get_workflow_results`; use `estimate_run`, agree costs, and `run_workflow`. If the run pauses with awaiting_approval (a step needs more than the approved max_usd), ask the person before `resume_run`.

## Per-scene narration is a different planner

The narration_edits parameter belongs to explainer.plan, not music_video.hud_plan. In an explainer workflow, read its script and scene IDs, then use `edit_workflow` with set_params on the explainer.plan node, for example params: {"narration_edits":{"s3":{"text":"Replacement narration.","was":"Previous narration."}}}. The keys are actual scene IDs; text is the replacement and was is the previous narration for a stale-edit check. Source-card scenes are excluded; stale or overlong edits are not applied. There is no HUD narration_edits parameter: change the song lyrics or brief through the actual HUD inputs instead.
