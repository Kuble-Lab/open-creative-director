# A singing guest in a HUD video
Use `music-video-hud-guest` for an uploaded duet song with its final lyrics. It extends `music-video-hud` with a second influencer and character sheet, with solo lead and guest clips sharing the performance lists and shared duet scenes using their own lists.

1. Call `list_templates` and `create_from_template`. Upload the song to n1.asset; supply the final lyrics at n3.lyrics. Set n34.influencer to the guest ID returned by `list_influencers` / `get_influencer`, or fill n4.guest with identity text in the same form as n4.figure (`NAME:`, `FULL:`, optional `SHORT:` and `CREDIT:`). An unselected influencer leaves the identity to the field.
2. Keep lead lines outside parentheses. Put each guest answer on its own whole line in round brackets, for example `(Not yet!)` or `(No.)`; [Choir] or a `Choir:` line prefix also marks the guest. Timing voice `choir` (including detected backing voices) is assigned to the guest only when a guest identity is given. The all-parentheses section exception still applies: read `get_guide` topic hud-music-video. Use [Choir] for a guest-only section.
3. n4.guest_sheet_prompt feeds the separate guest sheet n35, with n4.guest_sheet_refs as references. n34.images feeds n4.guest_image: up to six workflow PNG/JPEG/WebP images are accepted, the first three are used. The two image.generate sheets use the same image-model selector and 3:4 aspect. The guest sheet is the SECOND reference after the lead sheet on n9, n10 and n11; connection order matters. The text templates instruct the image model to draw only the named people, keeping each identity from its own sheet.
4. Short guest answers get their own performance units (down to about 1.2 s). Their audio starts exactly at the scene start and runs for at least 5.05 s for lip sync; silence pads the end of the song if necessary. The cut uses only the planned scene from the clip start at normal speed. Lead and guest remain in unit order in the same performance lists and use the same lip-sync node n12.
5. Review the brief, board, BOTH sheets and scene pictures with `get_workflow_results` before approving clips and HUD. Identity-preservation instructions still need visual review. Use `estimate_run`, agree costs, then `run_workflow`.

The guest identity is optional on music_video.hud_plan: without one its prompts and grid stay as before, and choir lines remain story clips. With `duet_scenes: auto` both duet outputs are present as empty lists even without a guest; with `off` they are absent. The duet template itself needs a guest identity because n35 has a required prompt input; without one it cannot finish. Use the original `music-video-hud` template for a solo film.

To retrofit another HUD workflow, first read `get_workflow` with include_graph and `list_node_types`. Keep n33, n4.figure, n5 and n12. Connect a second input.influencer's figure to n4.guest and images to n4.guest_image; wire guest_sheet_prompt / guest_sheet_refs to a second sheet. Add that sheet after n5 on each picture node's images input and adapt n6/n7/n8 to identify both references. Solo lead and guest units still share n12. For shared scenes also set `duet_scenes: auto` and add the reference-video branch below.

Close voice exchanges with gaps of at most 1 s now form shared duet scenes of 5 to 15 s within the same lip-sync budget: both singers stay in one frame and each sings only their lines. The guest template enables `duet_scenes: auto`; H3 Max reference generates these clips at its displayed per-second rate (768P: 0.08 USD/s, duration rounded up), plus reference tokens above the free allowance. To retrofit a workflow, connect duet_prompts to `fal.h3_reference.prompt`, duet_audio to its single `audio` input, the lead sheet then guest sheet to `images`, and `video` to `music_video.edit.duet`. Set `duration_from_audio: true` and `prompt_expansion: disabled`. The WAV spans exactly the scene without padding. Empty duet lists skip generation and cost nothing. Solo performance clips keep their separate consecutive indexes. The HUD keeps both faces clear; tags follow the larger stable face when voice identity is unknown.

In the template n37 is the reference-video node. Its clips belong to the same post-approval stage as n12, not to a new approval gate. No exchange means zero n37 runs; with `off` the planner omits both new outputs and preserves existing workflow cache keys. A duet shot is `kind: performance`, `voice: duet`, with its own `clip` index starting at 0; `parseShots.performance` excludes it and `parseShots.duet` counts it.
